Do large language models tell the same history in every language? This project audited four LLMs on contested questions about the Vietnam War, comparing responses across languages to look for nationalistic framing and cross-lingual inconsistency.

The paper ranked first in its AI course at Kenyon College and was invited for archiving on Kenyon's digital humanities platform.
