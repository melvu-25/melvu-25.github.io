I'm a first-year PhD student in Information Science at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Dr. Masha Krupenkin. I'm a member of the Computational Social Dynamics Lab.

My research sits in computational social science. I study how information spreads through social networks, how political communication plays out online, and how platform design shapes public discourse. I'm also interested in how large language models represent contested histories and perspectives across languages.

Before UMD, I graduated from Kenyon College in 2026 with a double major in Applied Mathematics/Statistics and Chinese. I've worked on machine learning in industry at VinBigdata in Vietnam, where I built a production pricing model on large-scale ride-share data, and I've done network analysis research on public discourse around local energy projects.

I work in English, Vietnamese, and Mandarin Chinese.

This site
------
I use this site to keep track of my research and to document the PhD as it happens. The [PhD Journal](/year-archive/) is where I write about what I'm reading, learning, and working through.

Research interests
------
* Social network analysis and information diffusion
* Political communication on social media
* Platform design and public discourse
* Cross-lingual bias in large language models
